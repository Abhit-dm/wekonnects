The Meeting Blueprint.

WeKonnects meetings are highly structured, energetic, and completely focused on generating revenue. Here is exactly what happens when the doors close.

The 6-Step Agenda

A proven formula designed to maximize your ROI every single week.

01

Open Networking

Building Relationships

The first 30 minutes are dedicated to informal mingling. This is your time to grab coffee, welcome visitors, and schedule 1-to-1 meetings with members you want to collaborate with.

02

30-Second Pitches

The Sales Force

Every member gets 30 seconds to train the room. You will explain exactly what your business does and specifically ask for the exact type of client or referral you want the group to find for you this week.

03

Feature Presentation

The Deep Dive

Each week, one selected member gets the floor for an extended 10-minute presentation. This is your opportunity to deeply educate the chapter on your industry, showcase success stories, and turn members into your active sales reps.

04

Super Minute

For The Super Stars

A high-energy, dedicated 60-second spotlight given to the superstars of the meeting. This rewards members who go above and beyond, giving them extra visibility to pitch an exclusive offer or highlight a massive success.

05

Passing Links & Deals

The ROI Moment

This is the most important part of the meeting. Members formally exchange warm referral links, announce closed business deals generated from the group, and publicly thank members for the revenue they’ve brought in.

06

Crowning the SOM

Star of the Member

We close the meeting by recognizing the absolute best performer. The Star of the Month (SOM) award is given to the member who has brought the most value, visitors, and closed business to the chapter, cementing their status as a top-tier networker.
