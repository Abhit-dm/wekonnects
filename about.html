The WeKonnects Standard

Our Core Values & Ethics

We do not accept just anyone. Our platform thrives because every member is held strictly to these four foundational pillars.

Trust & Integrity

We believe business should be done with a handshake you can count on. We strictly vet members to ensure ethical business practices.

Rigid Accountability

Talk is cheap. We track attendance, referrals, and closed business. If a member isn't contributing to the group's ROI, they lose their seat.

Total Exclusivity

One profession per chapter. By eliminating internal competition, we create a safe environment where members freely share their highest-value clients.

Givers Prosper

Our culture is built on giving first. We educate our members to constantly look out for business opportunities for others, knowing success will naturally return to them.
